GOOD QUESTIONS

A few things schools often want to know.

Is RAZET SMS suitable for both small and large schools?

RAZET SMS is designed to support school operations through configurable user roles and school-management workflows. The right subscription and setup should be selected based on your school's size and needs.

Who can use the platform?

School owners, management, teachers and other authorised staff can use their role-appropriate workspaces. Parent and student access is provided through their respective portal experiences where enabled.

Can parents access their child's information?

Parents can use the parent portal to access information made available to their linked account by the school. What they can see depends on the school's setup and the permissions assigned.

Can the school track outstanding fees?

Yes. Fee-management and fee-clearance workflows help the school review payments, outstanding balances and student clearance records, and support reminders.

Does RAZET SMS support staff payroll?

The platform includes payroll workflows for preparing payroll, school-owner approval, payment tracking and available staff payslips. Payment completion depends on the configured payment process and confirmation.

Can our school operate more than one branch?

Group and branch-management capabilities are available for supported multi-branch arrangements, subject to the required setup and permissions.

How do we subscribe?

Use the Get Started button to visit the RAZET SMS platform, register your school and review the available subscription options.
